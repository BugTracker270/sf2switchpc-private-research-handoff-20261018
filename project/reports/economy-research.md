# Currency representation and wallet arithmetic

Updated 2026-10-17T08:26:34.003364+00:00. Original ARM64 code and compiled research references; no real save was opened or changed.

Generic currency counters use a 16-byte ObscuredInt value: currentCryptoKey at +0, hiddenValue +4, fakeValue +8, inited byte +12. CurrencyStruct stores its currency reference at +0x10 and count at +0x18. Constructor, reference identity, aggregate getter/setter, XOR conversions, zero-key fallback and lazy initialization match 1024 cases / 15360 checks. Original code: constructor 0xD3D2C0, conversion pair 0x7926A0 / 0x794890, unboxed InternalDecrypt 0x7A8F20. Absent, inactive and active-consistent detector states are covered; the mismatch callback is outside scope.

Money and Bonus use separate 32-byte ObscuredLong values: 64-bit key/hidden/fake words at +0/+8/+0x10 and inited byte +0x18. Roster stores Money at +0x118, Bonus at +0x138, PaidMoney at +0x158 and PaidBonus at +0x178. The two decoded balance getters operate on copies, so lazy initialization there does not initialize the original roster field. Codecs, full Money/Bonus getters and the indirect-result PaidBonus getter match 1024 cases / 20480 checks, including signed boundaries and nonzero upper words. Original conversions 0x792BB0 / 0x7A1DD0, unboxed decrypt 0x7A9C70, getters 0xFD4B70 / 0xFD4C20 / 0xFD50B0. The +0x158 PaidMoney layout is observed in DenominateMoney; its complete getter/setter behavior is not certified by these checks.

SetMoney (0xFD0880) obtains pow(10, denominationDigits at roster +0x218), converts to int64 with ARM64 FCVTZS, and selects at least 1 as the persistence multiplier. The protected in-memory Money receives the supplied value; SaveAttribute receives that value multiplied by the multiplier with native 64-bit wraparound. The post-pow instructions through the SaveAttribute call arguments are tested; the imported pow implementation and XML persistence are not executed by this slice.

SetBonus (0xFD0990) always writes the new Bonus. Type 0 skips PaidBonus bookkeeping. For nonzero type, an increase with isPaid adds the absolute native difference to PaidBonus; an unpaid increase leaves it unchanged. A decrease subtracts max(0, delta - oldBonus + paidBonus), which consumes free bonus first for ordinary nonnegative valid balances. Native unchecked overflow behavior is retained in the research reference. Equal balances leave PaidBonus unchanged. Increased balances proceed to a statistics allocation after the balance writes.

The setter checks cover 2048 cases / 14336 comparisons. Their boundaries are explicit: SaveAttribute is observed then returned without executing XML persistence; increases stop before statistics allocation. These are arithmetic/dataflow checks, not a successful saved transaction. Original long Max at 0x663F30 is executed. The Money slice starts at 0xFD08D0 with a supplied pow result and stops at 0xFD0978 before SaveAttribute.

QuestActionGiveCurrency RunAction (0xD83520) obtains expression-derived type/value pairs (GetValues 0xD83760), then adds Money/Bonus as int64; Bonus uses reason 6 and isPaid=false. Generic currencies use an int32 conversion before AddCurrencyCount (0xFDE550). QuestActionTakeCurrency's native helper is 0xD901E0.
